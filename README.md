## Giovanni (Jonathan) Zingaro

PhD Candidate, Civil Engineering, University of Waterloo

Computational engineering and machine learning researcher who combines data-driven models with numerical simulation to build digital twins of dynamical systems. My work spans neural surrogates for coupled multiphysics simulation, 3D deep learning on industrial sensor data, and end-to-end machine learning tools adopted in industry.

### Research

- **Digital Twin of Cameco's Jet Boring System** (2023 – present): designed a 3D U-Net volumetric segmentation workflow that predicts cavity geometry and optimizes operational inputs. It forms the modelling core of an Offline Planning Tool used by mine engineers at the Cigar Lake Mine.
- **Geological Carbon Sequestration** (2022 – present): developed a stabilized finite element solver for coupled multiphase flow and poroelasticity, a deep learning monitoring framework for reservoir pressure, and reduced-order models and neural surrogates (PINN, FNO, U-Net, GNN) that accelerate simulation by several orders of magnitude.
